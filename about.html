How it works.

Wise people figure out complex things, forensically, for a fee, for people who are not so street smart. The home page is the signs. This page is the words behind them.

Request a sleuth

You describe the scenario — what happened, what you know, what you want found out — and name what you would pay. Someone who has read contracts for forty years, or done time and learned to see through things, digs through the record and tells you what it shows: a company, a claim, a deed, a court record, a scam, a family history, a property, a public file.

A sleuth bids; you pick one or none. You pay up front by card; the money is held and goes to the sleuth two days after the work is delivered. Always remote, all digital — the alternative to a private detective. What you get is a named person's opinion, never advice.

The club meets

A verified sleuth puts an event on — what, when, where, how many, and the price: walks, coffees, library days and evenings to dig together, each with $5 at least on the door so the people who come mean it. You register once (name, telephone, email, hometown, where you are now, a picture) and ask to come; the host decides who is in, and can bar anyone. Who is coming is shown on the event. Approval is a credit line, no interest, settled by bank transfer.
