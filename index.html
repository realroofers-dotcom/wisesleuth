Who the sleuths are

People who have seen it all: detectives who did it for a living, ex-convicts who know how it is done from the inside, seniors with decades of reading people, paper and deals, mystery readers who learned how it gets found out. Every one has a name, a face, a telephone and a town on file, says why they are good at this, and carries every review as written. Nobody is anonymous. Nothing is free.
