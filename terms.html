What you are buying

A sleuth's reading of your situation, for the fee you named and a sleuth accepted; a seat at a club event; the shirt. A gig is paid up front by card and held until the work is delivered; ask before then and it is simply returned.

Opinion, never advice

What a sleuth writes is one named person's opinion of what the record shows. It is not legal, financial, medical or investment advice and never claims to be. Always remote, all digital. Nobody is anonymous, on either side; everyone is reviewed, 140 characters, as written. All minds have value.

Cards go through Stripe. Stripe's page takes the card; we never see or store your card number. Your statement reads TS WISESLEUTH. Bank payments go through achplug.com, our own ACH software: you approve once, your bank sends it, no card company in the middle. Nothing is charged without your press.

We keep what a sale needs: your name, your email, what you bought, when, and what it cost. Where a site asks for more — a telephone, a town, a picture — the page says why before you type it. Nothing about you is sold, rented or handed to an advertiser. Every email we send has a way to stop it.
